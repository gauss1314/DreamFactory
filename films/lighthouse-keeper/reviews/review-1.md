# 审片意见

I went through the frames for all 9 shots in order (the s2, s7 and s9 shots twice, once full and once in the 1:1 close-ups), checking each against the brief and the art spec. The worst problems are in s7: the otter has nothing under it and the match isn't in its paw, and the window it should push shut is never pushed. Below are the 8 I'd send back, most serious first.

1. **s7 · full-45 / near-47 / full-50 (44.2–47.2 s): the otter is floating in mid-air, and the match isn't in its paw.** The brief says it climbs up onto the lamp stand. In the frames it just clings to the side of the lens drum, with nothing under its feet and its legs and tail hanging free. In near-47 the lit match floats on its own about 30 px below its feet. It isn't held in a paw and isn't shielded by its body, and it doesn't reach the wick in the middle of the lens. full-50 has the same floating pose.
2. **s7 · full-41 / near-42 (41.5–41.7 s): it never pushes the window shut.** The otter stands on the windowsill with its paws curled at its chest and its body clearly away from the window frame to its left. Nothing touches the frame. The window also looks already shut, so the "use its whole body to push the window shut and latch it" beat simply isn't there.
3. **s2 · full-04 / near-04 (8.0 s): it passes through the floor coming out of the hatch.** The hatch is roughly x 487–760, but the otter's body is mostly to the right of it (x 755–880). Its lower body is cut off by the floorboards, so it looks like it's stuck in the solid floor, not climbing up through the opening.
4. **s2 · full-07, 08 / full-10, 11 / near-12 (11.0–13.8 s): pouring the oil and wiping the lens are both missing.**
   - **Pouring:** the oil can's spout touches the brass ring on the outside of the drum. There's no oil trough and no stream of oil.
   - **Wiping:** both paws press on the brass collar below the lens. There's no corner of the scarf and no contact with the glass.
5. **s5 · full-28 to full-33 (28.7–32.95 s): the window that blows open is a frame floating in the room.** At x≈470–670 a window frame hangs in mid-air in front of the parapet. It has no hinge and isn't attached to any opening. The glass and frame bars behind it are still whole, so you can't tell which window blew open.
6. **s3 · full-17/18 (17.8–18.0 s): the lightning flash isn't lit, and the otter is looking the wrong way.** At the flash its face is still evenly lit warm, with no cold blue light from the side. It has turned to face the camera head-on, when the brief has it look out the window.
7. **s9 · full-59 to 66 (60.0–64.15 s): the main beats are too small to read and the wave isn't drawn.**
   - **Too small:** the otter is only about 130 px tall. Its wet fur and its squint can't be seen.
   - **No wave:** after the horn sounds the second time (near-61, near-63), both paws are still under its chin. No paw is ever raised.
   - **No footing:** in near-63 and 66 its back legs and tail hang in the air with nothing to stand on.
8. **s4 · full-21 / full-25 (22.5–24.7 s): the big wave doesn't hit the rocks, and the lightning lights nothing.** *(should)*
   - **Wave:** the three white splashes (x 420–1050) all sit on open water to the right of the foreground rock. They are three similar splashes that look pasted on.
   - **Lightning:** in full-21 the bolt runs straight down onto the horizon, but the clouds, waves and tower are no brighter than before.

Two more I saw but left out of the 8:
- **s8:** in full-56 the light beam is a flat band, the same width all the way across the frame, that doesn't start at the lamp. The boat's wake is a white blob, not an arc.
- **s9:** the lens in the lamp room has turned into an oval paper-lantern shape, which doesn't match the straight brass drum in s2 and s7.
